# Limitations

- Civilization VI is a bounded game environment, not a national-security
  decision environment.
- Game outcomes do not establish policy correctness or general strategic
  competence.
- The game has no authoritative policy answer key.
- Runtime, engine, model, prompt, tool, and scenario choices can affect every
  result.
- Current private qualification evidence is not equivalent to a completed
  live scientific campaign.
- No Chinese-model geopolitical finding is established here.
- Sample size, power, model matrix, and held-out scenarios remain future
  design decisions.
- A successful game result cannot compensate for an unsafe action, invalid
  authority, unverified completion, or incomplete evidence.
- The candidate does not distribute Civilization VI assets, saves, raw
  captures, raw provider responses, or private operational records.
